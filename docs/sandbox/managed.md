# The AgentNode Sandbox

> **Status: planned. Not available, not bookable, not priced.**
> Nothing on this page can be used today. It exists so you can see the intended shape and tell us
> whether it fits, before it is built.

*For everyone.*

## The idea

You click, you pick your files, it runs. The sealed machine is ours; nothing is installed on yours.
That is the point — the complicated part stays with us.

## The intended flow

1. **Choose a region.** For people in the EU, an EU region is preselected.
2. **See what will be sent.** In plain language: which files, where they go, how long anything is
   kept, and what it may cost.
3. **Agree once.** Not on every run — once, and again only if something material changes: a
   different region, a different operator, different data, a longer retention, or changed terms.
4. **See your free allowance.** The intention is a small number of free runs with **no payment
   method required**, so the first thing you meet is not a payment form.
5. **Run it.**

After that, ordinary runs do not ask again.

## What we intend to promise

* **one sealed machine per job**, with its own kernel — not a shared box with walls drawn on it;
* **your keys would never enter it.** The sandbox gets a placeholder; a broker outside substitutes the
  real value only for destinations you allowed;
* **the workspace and any temporary access are destroyed** as soon as you have your result;
* **logs containing your content live at most 24 hours** by default; audit records without content
  live 30 days;
* **you can withdraw consent** in one place, and the next run stops rather than continuing quietly;
* **a spending ceiling you cannot accidentally exceed**, and free meaning free — no card, no
  surprise.

## What has not been decided

Prices. Dates. Which regions exist at launch. Where the free allowance ends and paid use begins.
Anything here about cost is an intention, not an offer.

We would rather say that than invent a number.

## What you can do today

Nothing on this page — that is the honest answer. If you want the protection now and you are on a
computer, [set up the local sandbox](setup-local.md): it is free, it works, and the work stays on your
machine.

If this page is what you actually want, the useful thing is to tell us which part matters most — the
region, the key handling, the retention, or simply not having to install anything.
